Medication Timer

Tap a medication when it is taken. Oxy tracks 5 mg tablets individually with a 4-hour minimum per 5 mg slot and a 6-tablet / 30 mg daily maximum. Oxy and Robaxin are kept at least 1 hour apart.

Important: This app uses the timing schedule selected for this medication tracker. If a prescription label, discharge instruction, pharmacist, or clinician gives different timing or daily limits, follow those instructions instead.
Background alerts: Enable them to receive reminders when the app is closed. Tap Disable Background Alerts at any time to stop push reminders without changing the medication log or timers.
